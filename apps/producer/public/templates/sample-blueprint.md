# Global Hiring Blueprint — Senior Full-Stack Engineer

## 1. The Job Posting

### Senior Full-Stack Engineer

**Department:** Engineering  
**Location:** Remote (EMEA)  
**Employment Type:** Full-time

#### About the Role

We are looking for a **Senior Full-Stack Engineer** to join our growing engineering team. You will design and build scalable web applications, collaborate with cross-functional teams, and mentor junior engineers.

#### What You'll Do

- Architect and build production-grade web applications using modern frameworks
- Design and optimize database schemas and API endpoints
- Participate in code reviews and establish engineering best practices
- Collaborate with Product and Design to deliver exceptional user experiences
- Mentor junior team members and contribute to engineering culture

#### Requirements

- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong proficiency in **TypeScript**, **React**, and **Node.js**
- Experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL)
- Familiarity with cloud services (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Excellent communication skills in English

#### Nice to Have

- Experience with Next.js and server-side rendering
- Familiarity with containerization (Docker, Kubernetes)
- Open-source contributions
