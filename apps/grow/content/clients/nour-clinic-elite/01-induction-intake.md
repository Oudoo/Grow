## What the playbook already knows

The playbook is unusually specific about *how* to run this meeting, which tells
us what has already been judged about the client:

**A surgical practice.** The capture fields ask for "real surgical volume and
pricing corrections" — so this is surgery, not general practice. The exact
specialty is never stated in the playbook and should not be guessed.

**Numbers we hold are expected to be wrong.** The approach is to slide a printed
Predictive Forecast across the table and *"let him correct your numbers."* Treat
every figure currently on file as unverified until he has corrected it — that is
the explicit design of the meeting, not a caveat.

**Aggregators are the live objection.** The playbook prepares for the
*"Vezeeta / booking app objection"*, with the counter that such platforms
**commoditise his legacy**. This is the single most consequential thing in the
file: it means the strategic choice — owned demand capture versus integrating
with platforms he already sells through — is contested, and he has a position on
it. Do not present an aggregator-adjacent solution without addressing this.

**Green Lab integration** is a named topic with prepared talking points. What
Green Lab is, and what integrating with it entails, is not recorded — establish
this before the meeting.

**The close is a "14-Day Quick Win."** The meeting is designed to end by
establishing authority and defining one fast, concrete win. Have a candidate
ready that does not depend on data we do not yet have.
