# Master Intelligence & Strategic Framework

**Client:** 180 Dental & Cosmetics · **Prepared by:** GROW Ecosystem

> Source note: this document is the strategic framework as prepared from client
> discovery and meeting intelligence. Claims here are **client-reported** unless
> stated otherwise. Independently verified observations are kept separate, in
> *Digital Footprint Audit — 180.clinic*, so the two never get confused.

## Phase 1 — Digital footprint & brand foundation

### Business architecture

- **Business model:** Hybrid. B2C (high-ticket dental and cosmetic patients) and
  B2B (sub-leasing clinical chairs to external doctors).
- **Industry:** Healthcare / cosmetic dentistry & aesthetics.
- **Core infrastructure:** Fully equipped facility with an **in-house lab
  capable of same-day crowns** — a significant competitive moat.

### Customer segments

- **B2C:** Premium local patients plus a lucrative **expat demographic
  (~30% of current base)**.
- **B2B:** Independent dentists and cosmetic practitioners needing access to a
  high-end facility.

### Brand maturity: infrastructure-rich, system-poor

180 Dental has invested heavily in top-tier medical hardware and physical space
but lacks the digital infrastructure to fill it. The result is a **feast-or-famine
cycle** — days with zero patients — forcing reliance on B2B chair rental simply
to cover baseline overhead.

## Phase 2 — The operational bottleneck

Three failure points the Grow Ecosystem must solve:

1. **The founder trap.** Dr. Salsabil is personally stuck in Instagram DMs. The
   lead clinician is acting as the front-desk sales rep, which caps clinical
   capacity and burns out the primary revenue generator.
2. **Price-objection drop-offs.** High-ticket leads arrive but disappear the
   moment price is revealed. There is no value-building or price-anchoring
   sequence before the number is given.
3. **Severe underutilisation.** Days with zero patients. A state-of-the-art lab
   and empty chairs are depreciating assets.

## Phase 3 — Information gap mapping

| Category | Verified intelligence | The gap — what we still need |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| B2C patient flow | 30% expat base; heavy drop-off at price | Exact lead-to-consultation conversion rate. How many DMs equal one booked chair? |
| B2B doctor flow | Chair rental covers baseline expenses | Exact hourly/daily margin on a rented chair vs. an owned B2C procedure |
| Tech stack | Manual DM follow-up by Dr. Salsabil | Current EMR/booking software. Is there a digital calendar for B2B chair rental? |
| Clinical margin | Same-day crowns (high margin, fast turnaround) | Exact COGS for the in-house lab vs. outsourcing |

## Phase 4 — Dual-sided solution architecture

180 Dental runs two businesses under one roof, so the architecture is dual-sided.

### Pipeline A — B2C high-ticket patient engine

**Objective:** get Dr. Salsabil out of the DMs, and pre-frame price.

- Automated DM routing (ManyChat → CRM). When a patient asks for price, the
  system delivers a high-production video of Dr. Salsabil **in the in-house lab**
  explaining why same-day crowns are premium — anchoring value before revealing
  cost.
- Automated follow-up SMS sequences targeting the expat community with
  localised, English-first copy.

### Pipeline B — B2B "chair fill" automation

**Objective:** eliminate zero-revenue days by systematising chair rental.

- A closed-loop booking portal for external doctors.
- Doctors view live chair availability, book slots, and **pay the rental fee
  upfront** through the portal — removing the administrative burden from 180's
  front desk entirely.

## Phase 5 — Strategic induction focus

The narrative with Dr. Salsabil and 180 management is
**"reclaiming your time and maximising your assets."**

> "Dr. Salsabil, you have built a world-class facility with an in-house lab. You
> are a highly skilled practitioner, but right now you are acting as an
> administrative assistant in your own DMs. The Grow Ecosystem is going to build
> a dual-engine CRM: one that automatically handles patient price objections so
> you only speak to ready-to-buy clients, and a second automated calendar that
> keeps your empty chairs rented to other doctors without you lifting a finger."

## Open dependencies

Before Pipeline A or B can be scoped for build, the four gaps in Phase 3 need
answers, and the issues in *Digital Footprint Audit — 180.clinic* need
resolving — several of them block lead capture regardless of what automation
sits behind it.
