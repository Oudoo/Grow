## Phase 2 — Operational bottleneck

**UNKNOWN — nothing verified yet.** Questions to answer:

- Where do leads currently arrive: Instagram, WhatsApp, phone, walk-in, referral?
- Who answers them, and how long does a first reply take?
- Is there a booking system, or is scheduling manual?
- What is the drop-off point — price, availability, location, or scheduling friction?
- **Seasonality:** the North Coast location is at a resort. Is Almaza Bay a
  summer-only operation? If so, how is the off-season covered, and does Sheikh
  Zayed carry the year? This is the single most consequential unknown, because
  it changes the entire shape of a marketing calendar.

## Phase 4 — Solution architecture

**Cannot be designed until Phases 2 and 3 are answered.** Premature architecture
is how you build the wrong system confidently.

Two things are already evident from the audit and should proceed regardless:

1. **Fix inbound email** — the published address bounces (audit finding #1).
2. **Resolve the two-domain split** — it fragments SEO and caused #1.
